# Third-party notices / Licences tierces

The Android app bundles its own MIT license as
`app/public/common-foundry-license.txt` and provides three offline notice pages:

- `app/public/rust-licenses.html`: license texts and attributions for the
  Android ARM64 Rust runtime graph, generated by cargo-about from the locked
  Tauri workspace using `app/src-tauri/about.toml` and `about.hbs`.
- `app/public/javascript-licenses.html`: license texts for all six production
  JavaScript packages reported by `pnpm licenses list --json --prod`.
- `app/public/android-library-notices.html`: the 106 resolved ARM release
  Maven runtime artifacts, their published POM licenses or terms, the Apache
  2.0 text, and the libyuv BSD notice used by CameraX. The two artifacts with
  no license in their own POM inherit Apache 2.0 from their published parents;
  the parent coordinates are recorded in the inventory.

The QR scanner uses Google ML Kit. Google says camera input and scan results
are processed on the device, but ML Kit may contact Google for updates and
sends performance and usage metrics. The Android notice page links to the
[ML Kit terms and privacy information](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).
Distribution and privacy disclosures for these Google components require
review for the intended release channel.

Source inventory: `license-inventory/gradle-arm-release.json`. Regenerate the
JavaScript and Android notice pages with the two scripts in `scripts/` after
changing dependencies. Regenerate the Rust page with cargo-about using the
checked-in config and template. Verify all three pages are present in the
final APK before publishing.

La page Android signale aussi que ML Kit peut envoyer des mesures d'utilisation
et de performance à Google. Les images et résultats du scan restent traités
sur l'appareil selon la documentation de Google.
